Echo service has been deployed!

1. Access the application:
  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "echo-service.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

  Then visit http://localhost:8080

2. Check pod status:
  kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/name={{ include "echo-service.name" . }}

3. View logs:
  kubectl logs -l app.kubernetes.io/name={{ include "echo-service.name" . }}
